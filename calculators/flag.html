What Is a Flag Bet? The 23-Bet System Explained

A flag bet combines four selections into 23 separate wagers: six doubles, four trebles and one four-fold accumulator. This structure means a £1 unit stake produces a £23 total stake (or £46 for Each Way flag bets). Each component bet operates independently—doubles require two selections to win, trebles need three winners, and the four-fold demands all four selections succeed. The Flag Bet Calculator processes these 23 bets simultaneously, calculating returns for every possible winning combination within the four selections.

The flag format offers coverage across multiple outcomes. When all four selections win, all 23 bets succeed, producing maximum returns from the doubles, trebles and four-fold combined. If only three selections win, the corresponding treble and three doubles succeed while other bets fail. Two winners trigger one double but leave all trebles and the four-fold unsuccessful. This graduated structure provides returns even when not all selections win, unlike a straight four-fold accumulator that requires complete success.

Odds formats interact with the 23-bet structure through multiplication. Fractional odds convert to multipliers for each bet combination—a 3/1 winner becomes a 4x multiplier when calculating returns. Decimal odds represent the total return per unit staked, including the original stake. American odds convert to either fractional or decimal equivalents before the Flag Bet Calculator processes the 23 individual bets. Each Way selections double the bet count to 46 by placing both win and place bets across all 23 combinations, with place odds typically paid at one-fifth or one-quarter of win odds depending on event conditions.

Rule 4 deductions reduce returns when non-runners withdraw after bets are placed, applying official percentage reductions to remaining selections. Dead Heat rules divide returns when selections tie, with half returns for two-way ties or third returns for three-way ties. The Flag Bet Calculator applies these adjustments across all affected bets within the 23-bet structure automatically.

Breaking Down the 23 Bets

The six doubles pair selections in all possible combinations: A+B, A+C, A+D, B+C, B+D and C+D. Each double requires both selections to win, with returns calculated by multiplying the two odds together then applying the unit stake. When using Decimal odds, a 2.00 selection combined with a 3.00 selection produces a 6.00 double (total return of £6 per £1 staked).

The four trebles combine three selections: A+B+C, A+B+D, A+C+D and B+C+D. Treble returns multiply three odds together—significantly larger payouts than doubles but requiring an additional winning selection. Fractional odds of 2/1, 3/1 and 4/1 produce a treble multiplier of 3 × 4 × 5 = 60x the unit stake.

The single four-fold accumulator combines all selections A+B+C+D. This bet delivers the largest return within the flag structure but fails completely if any selection loses. American odds convert before multiplication—four selections at +200 each convert to 3.00 decimal odds, producing a four-fold return of 81.00 (£81 per £1 staked). The Flag Bet Calculator displays each bet component separately, showing which doubles, trebles and the four-fold contribute to total returns based on actual results.

Each Way Flag Bets in Horse Racing

Each Way flag bets double the total stake by placing both win and place components on all 23 bets within the structure. With 4 selections creating 6 doubles, 4 trebles, and a four-fold accumulator, the Each Way version generates 46 separate bets total. Place terms typically pay 1/4 or 1/5 of the win odds for horses finishing in the top three or four positions, depending on field size and race type.

Horse racing supports Each Way betting more comprehensively than other sports, making it the primary context for flag bet calculations. When a selection places but does not win, the place portion of all related bets still returns at the reduced odds fraction. For example, if a horse quoted at 8/1 in Fractional odds finishes third with 1/5 place terms, the place bet pays at 8/5 (1.60 in Decimal odds or +160 in American odds).

The Flag Bet Calculator processes these dual components by calculating win returns separately from place returns across all 23 combinations. A £1 Each Way flag costs £46 total stake. If two selections win and two place, the returns combine winnings from completed accumulators with partial returns from place-only outcomes.

Place terms vary by race conditions. Handicap races with sixteen or more runners often pay 1/4 odds for first through fourth place. Non-handicap races with five to seven runners typically pay 1/5 odds for first through third only. Rule 4 deductions and Dead Heat provisions apply to both win and place components when triggered by late withdrawals or tied finishes.

Football markets covering events like the Premier League rarely offer Each Way options, keeping this betting structure primarily within horse racing and occasionally greyhound racing contexts. How Rule 4 Deductions Affect Returns

Rule 4 deductions apply percentage reductions to winning odds based on the withdrawn horse's price at removal time. A horse withdrawn at 5/1 in Fractional odds triggers approximately 25 pence in the pound deduction, reducing a 7/1 winner to effective odds of 9/2. The Flag Bet Calculator applies this reduction to all bet legs containing the affected selection.

For example, a £1 flag with one selection at 7/1 that wins after a 25p Rule 4 deduction pays at adjusted odds of 9/2 (5.50 in Decimal odds or +450 in American odds). This impacts returns across all combinations including that selection. Multiple Rule 4 deductions in a single race compound the reductions proportionally.

Dead Heat Calculations

Dead Heat rules divide both stake and winnings by the number of tied selections. In a two-way Dead Heat for first place, each bet settles at half stake with half the projected winnings. A £2 stake at 6/1 Fractional odds becomes £1 stake returning £4 total (£3 profit plus £1 returned stake).

When flag bet selections tie, all related doubles, trebles, and the accumulator adjust proportionally. The Flag Bet Calculator factors Dead Heat reductions into Each Way place calculations where ties for third or fourth position frequently occur. A three-way Dead Heat divides returns by three, significantly reducing the total settlement across all 4 selections within the flag structure.

Converting Between Odds Formats

Fractional odds express profit relative to stake. A 5/1 selection returns £5 profit for every £1 wagered. To convert fractional to decimal, divide the numerator by denominator and add 1 (5/1 becomes 6.00). Decimal odds include the stake in the total return figure, simplifying payout calculations for multi-leg bets.

American odds use positive numbers for underdogs (+500 means £500 profit on a £100 stake) and negative numbers for favourites (-200 requires £200 stake to win £100 profit). Flag Bet Calculator handles these conversions internally when calculating potential returns.
