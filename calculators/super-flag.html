The 46 Bet Combinations

The Super Flag generates combinations through mathematical permutation of five selections:

  1. 10 Doubles - Every possible two-selection pairing from the five choices creates 10 separate double bets
  2. 10 Trebles - All three-selection combinations produce 10 treble accumulators
  3. 5 Fourfolds - Each four-selection grouping forms 5 fourfold accumulators
  4. 1 Fivefold - The single combination using all five selections
  5. 10 Single Stakes About - Ten conditional two-selection bets with protective stakes

These 46 bets mean that even if only two selections win, returns generate from the successful double bet. Three winners trigger multiple doubles and one treble. Four winners activate doubles, trebles, and a fourfold. All five winning produces returns across every bet type.

Understanding Single Stakes About Bets

Single Stakes About (SSA) bets form the distinguishing feature of Flag bet structures, adding 10 bets beyond standard accumulator combinations. Each SSA pairs two selections with conditional staking: if the first selection wins, its returns stake the second selection automatically. This creates a protective layer where one winner from a pair still generates return through the conditional stake.

The 10 SSA bets pair each selection with every other selection twice (once in each direction), producing 10 total combinations from 5 selections. This component explains why a Super Flag contains 46 bets rather than the 36 found in other five-selection accumulators. The calculator accounts for SSA returns separately, adding them to standard combination payouts.
