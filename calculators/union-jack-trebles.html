Dead Heat and Rule 4 Scenarios in Union Jack Bets

Dead Heat situations divide stakes by the number of winners sharing a position, directly affecting union jack calculator returns. Rule 4 deductions occur when non-runners withdraw after bet placement, reducing odds on remaining selections.
