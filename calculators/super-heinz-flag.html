Single Stakes About Insurance Component

The 42 Single Stakes About bets differentiate the Flag from standard accumulators. Each selection pairs with every other selection in both directions, creating 21 pairs multiplied by 2 directional combinations equals 42 SSA bets. If one selection from a pair wins and the other loses, the stake on the losing selection transfers to the winning selection as an additional single bet. This insurance mechanism protects against total loss when horses finish in mixed positions, recovering stake from losing selections to boost returns on winners. Place Terms and How They Work

Place odds calculate as a fraction of win odds, typically 1/4 or 1/5 depending on the bookmaker and race type. If a horse offers 4/1 win odds with 1/4 place terms, the place odds equal 4/1 divided by 4, resulting in 1/1 (evens). For 5/1 win odds at 1/5 place terms, place odds become 5/1 divided by 5, equaling 1/1. The number of paid places varies by field size in horse racing: races with 5-7 runners typically pay 2 places, 8-15 runners pay 3 places, and 16 or more runners pay 4 places. Handicap races often extend place terms to accommodate larger fields, with some bookmakers offering extra place promotions on major race days.
