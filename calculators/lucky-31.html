What Is a Lucky 31 Bet?

A Lucky 31 bet consists of 31 separate bets generated from 5 selections. The bet type covers every possible combination: 5 singles, 10 doubles, 10 trebles, 5 four-fold accumulators and 1 five-fold accumulator. This structure means at least one selection must win for a return. The name 'lucky' reflects the safety net built into the system. The Lucky 31 format originated in horse racing betting but has expanded to football, tennis and other sports. Each of the 5 selections generates multiple bets simultaneously.
