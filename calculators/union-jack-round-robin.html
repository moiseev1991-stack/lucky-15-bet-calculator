What Is a Union Jack Round Robin Bet?

Union Jack Round Robin is a betting system combining 9 selections into 80 separate bets through a distinctive grid structure. The bet takes its name from the British flag pattern, as the 9 selections arrange in a 3x3 grid with winning combinations following the same directional lines visible on the Union Jack flag.

The structure differs from standard round robin bets by imposing geometric organization on the selections. Players place their 9 selections into grid positions, and the system generates round robins from every horizontal row, vertical column, and both diagonal lines. This creates 8 round robins total—three from rows running left to right, three from columns running top to bottom, and two from the diagonal lines crossing through the centre position.

Each round robin contains 12 bets, covering doubles and trebles formed from the three selections in that particular line. The multiplication of 8 round robins by 10 bets per round robin produces the 80 total bets that make up the Union Jack Round Robin. All 9 selections contribute to multiple round robins simultaneously due to the overlapping nature of rows, columns and diagonals.

The bet type offers coverage across multiple outcomes without requiring all selections to win. Because the system splits into 8 independent round robins, partial success still generates returns. Union Jack vs Trixie

A Trixie bet uses three selections to create four separate bets: three doubles and one treble. Union Jack vs Yankee

A Yankee bet comprises four selections forming 11 bets: six doubles, four trebles, and one four-fold accumulator. This middle-ground option costs 11 times the unit stake, sitting between a Trixie and Union Jack in complexity. The Yankee requires fewer selections than the Union Jack but offers more coverage than a Trixie.
