Understanding the Super Yankee: 26 Bets from 5 Selections

A Super Yankee, also known as a Canadian, consists of 26 separate bets derived from 5 selections. The structure includes 10 doubles, 10 trebles, 5 four-fold accumulators, and 1 five-fold accumulator. At least 2 selections must win for any return, though maximum profit requires all 5 selections to succeed. This betting format offers multiple routes to returns while spreading risk across different combination types.

The breakdown of the 26 bets demonstrates the comprehensive coverage of this betting system. Each double pairs two selections, each treble combines three, each four-fold accumulator groups four selections, and the single five-fold accumulator includes all five picks. This structure means that even if only 2 or 3 selections win, returns are still possible through the smaller combinations, though the payout will be significantly lower than if all selections succeed.

The minimum stake requirement multiplies by 26, as each bet within the Canadian requires its own unit stake. For example, a £1 unit stake produces a total stake of £26. The potential returns depend on the odds of each selection and how many selections win. With all 5 selections winning, all 26 bets contribute to the total return, maximising profit potential.
